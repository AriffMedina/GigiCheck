## Prompt

Use case: ui-mockup. Create ONE exceptionally polished, high-resolution horizontal product-design presentation image containing FOUR large, complete iPhone screen mockups, side by side at identical scale, front-facing, no perspective, no overlap. This is a coherent redesign of GigiCheck, a personal Spanish-language pharmacy missing-item counting PWA for iPhone. Use the supplied image ONLY as a reference for brand identity, task flow and sample products; completely refine its interface. Preserve dark violet ink and lavender, but give it the precision, warmth, restraint and effortless usability of a beautifully crafted Apple product. This is an independent app, not an Apple-branded product.

Composition: a quiet neutral pearl-gray studio backdrop, extremely subtle soft shadows, four slim dark titanium iPhones with matching clean visible borders and discreet Dynamic Islands. Equal gutters and generous but economical outer margins. UI fills most of each device. All four devices fully visible, no clipped edges. Small neutral captions below: “Conteo”, “Foto”, “Revisión”, “PDF”. A modest presentation title outside devices: “GigiCheck” and “Tinta violeta · Rediseño”. The screens themselves are the hero. Maximize text sharpness and real mobile readability, not a decorative collage of tiny screens. Aim for 3840 × 2160 presentation quality.

Design bible across all four screens: iOS-premium, dark matte ink #100D17; soft elevated plum-charcoal #211B2B; off-white #F8F4FC; quiet lavender-gray supporting text; refined light lavender #C9ACE8 for the primary action with dark ink labels. Use color sparingly and functionally. No saturated purple gradients. No hard bright-purple outlines around every item. Separators are faint, warm-gray hairlines. SF Pro-like humanist system typography, realistic 17pt-equivalent body copy, clear semibold headings, tabular figures. One elegant editorial serif accent only on home “Todo en orden.” and PDF title. Comfortable 24pt-equivalent lateral margins, clear 8pt spacing rhythm, generous touch areas, 54–56pt primary buttons, continuous rounded corners and restrained shadows. A single subtle frosted navigation surface is allowed, never layers of glass cards. Custom refined SF-symbol-like icons, no emoji. Correct iPhone status bars at 9:41, proper safe areas and home indicators. No content under system chrome or buttons hidden behind navigation. Calm, useful, uncluttered, credible and easy to use with one hand.
